## Summary

Elderly patients should generally avoid taking calcium chloride injections concomitantly with calcium channel blockers or digoxin, as significant drug interactions can occur. If co-administration is unavoidable, close monitoring of blood pressure (for calcium channel blockers) or ECG (for digoxin) is required due to risks of reduced antihypertensive effect and synergistic arrhythmias, respectively [1].

---

## Clinical Notes

- **Calcium Channel Blockers:** Concomitant use of calcium chloride injection and calcium channel blockers may reduce the response to the antihypertensive medication. Avoid this combination if possible. If unavoidable, monitor blood pressure closely during administration [1].
- **Digoxin:** Avoid using calcium chloride injection with digoxin due to the risk of synergistic arrhythmias and increased risk of digoxin toxicity, especially if hypercalcemia develops. If co-administration cannot be avoided, monitor ECG closely during administration [1].
- **Other Drugs Increasing Hypercalcemia Risk:** If calcium chloride is used with drugs that increase the risk of hypercalcemia (e.g., calcipotriene, estrogen, lithium, parathyroid hormone, teriparatide, thiazide diuretics, Vitamin A, Vitamin D), increase the frequency of monitoring serum calcium concentrations [1].
- **Monitoring Parameters:** 
  - For patients on digoxin: Continuous ECG monitoring during calcium chloride administration.
  - For patients on calcium channel blockers: Frequent blood pressure monitoring during administration.
  - For all patients at risk of hypercalcemia: Regular serum calcium checks; increase frequency if using additional agents that raise calcium levels [1].
- **Missing Information:** The provided context does not address oral calcium supplements or other classes of blood pressure medications (e.g., ACE inhibitors, beta-blockers, ARBs, diuretics other than thiazides). The evidence is specific to calcium chloride injection and does not cover all possible calcium preparations or all antihypertensive agents [1], [2].
