**Interaction between Calcium and Lisinopril**

1. **Lisinopril Overview:**
   - Lisinopril is an angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor used primarily for hypertension, heart failure, and post-myocardial infarction management.
   - It works by inhibiting ACE, leading to vasodilation, reduced aldosterone secretion, and decreased blood pressure.

2. **Calcium (Supplement or Dietary) Overview:**
   - Calcium is an essential mineral important for bone health, muscle function, nerve transmission, and vascular contraction.
   - Calcium supplements are often taken for osteoporosis prevention or treatment, or to correct hypocalcemia.

3. **Potential Interactions:**
   - **Calcium and Lisinopril do not have a direct pharmacokinetic interaction.** Calcium does not significantly affect the absorption, metabolism, or excretion of lisinopril.
   - However, **calcium supplements (especially calcium carbonate) can sometimes affect blood pressure control** by potentially increasing vascular resistance, but this effect is generally mild.
   - **Lisinopril can increase serum potassium levels** by reducing aldosterone secretion. Calcium itself does not raise potassium but calcium supplements should be monitored in patients with renal impairment or those on multiple medications affecting electrolytes.
   - **Calcium channel blockers (a different class of antihypertensives) can interact with lisinopril, but calcium supplements do not have this effect.**

4. **Clinical Considerations:**
   - If a patient is taking both lisinopril and calcium supplements, routine monitoring of blood pressure and renal function is recommended.
   - Monitor serum electrolytes, especially potassium and calcium, in patients with renal impairment or those on multiple medications.
   - Advise patients to take calcium supplements and lisinopril at different times if concerned about absorption, although no strict timing is usually necessary.

5. **Summary:**
   - No significant direct drug interaction between calcium supplements and lisinopril.
   - Monitor blood pressure, renal function, and electrolytes as per usual clinical practice.
   - Adjust therapy based on clinical response and laboratory findings.
