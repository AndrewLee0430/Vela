## Summary

Elderly patients should generally avoid taking calcium chloride injections concomitantly with blood pressure (BP) medications, specifically calcium channel blockers, due to the risk of reduced antihypertensive efficacy. If concomitant use is unavoidable, close monitoring of blood pressure is required during administration of calcium chloride injection[1].

---

## Clinical Notes

- **Safety Warnings & Contraindications:**  
  - Concomitant use of calcium chloride injection with calcium channel blockers should be avoided because calcium chloride may reduce the response to these antihypertensive agents[1].
  - If co-administration cannot be avoided, blood pressure should be monitored closely during the administration of calcium chloride injection[1].
  - Calcium chloride injection should also be avoided with digoxin due to the risk of synergistic arrhythmias and increased risk of digoxin toxicity from hypercalcemia[1].
  - Use caution with other drugs that increase the risk of hypercalcemia (e.g., calcipotriene, estrogen, lithium, parathyroid hormone, teriparatide, thiazide diuretics, Vitamin A, and Vitamin D); increase the frequency of calcium concentration monitoring in these cases[1].

- **Monitoring Parameters:**  
  - Monitor blood pressure closely if calcium chloride injection is used with calcium channel blockers[1].
  - Monitor ECG closely if used with digoxin[1].
  - Increase frequency of serum calcium monitoring if used with other agents that raise calcium levels[1].

- **Missing Information:**  
  - The provided context does not address oral calcium supplements or other classes of BP medications besides calcium channel blockers and digoxin. There is also no specific data on elderly populations or chronic use in the provided references.

- **Mechanism:**  
  - Calcium chloride can antagonize the effects of calcium channel blockers, reducing their antihypertensive efficacy, and may increase the risk of hypercalcemia and related complications when used with other drugs affecting calcium metabolism[1].
