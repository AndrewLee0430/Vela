## Summary

Elderly patients can take blood pressure medications (such as hydrochlorothiazide) with calcium, but careful monitoring is required due to potential drug interactions and risks of electrolyte disturbances, particularly hypokalemia and possible effects on calcium metabolism[1]. Hydrochlorothiazide may intensify electrolyte depletion, and thiazides can affect laboratory tests related to parathyroid function, which is closely linked to calcium regulation[1].

---

## Clinical Notes

- **Safety Warnings and Contraindications:**  
  - Hydrochlorothiazide should be used with caution in elderly patients, especially those at risk for electrolyte imbalances or with impaired renal function[1].
  - Thiazide diuretics can cause or worsen hypokalemia, which may be exacerbated by corticosteroids or ACTH[1].
  - Thiazides should be discontinued before parathyroid function tests due to potential interference with calcium metabolism[1].
  - Use caution if the patient is also taking lithium, as thiazides can increase the risk of lithium toxicity[1].

- **Drug Interactions:**  
  - Calcium supplements are not specifically contraindicated, but thiazides can increase serum calcium levels by reducing renal calcium excretion, potentially leading to hypercalcemia, especially in patients with underlying parathyroid disorders[1].
  - Monitor for additive hypotensive effects if combined with other antihypertensive agents, alcohol, barbiturates, or narcotics[1].
  - Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) may reduce the antihypertensive and diuretic effects of hydrochlorothiazide; monitor blood pressure and renal function closely if used together[1].

- **Monitoring Parameters:**  
  - Monitor serum electrolytes (potassium, sodium, calcium) at baseline and periodically during therapy, especially in the elderly and those on calcium supplements[1].
  - Assess renal function (e.g., serum creatinine, eGFR) at baseline and regularly during treatment.
  - Watch for signs of volume depletion, orthostatic hypotension, and electrolyte disturbances.
  - If parathyroid function testing is planned, discontinue thiazides beforehand to avoid confounding results[1].
