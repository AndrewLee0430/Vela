## Summary  
Elderly patients can take blood pressure medications that include calcium channel blockers, but caution is necessary due to the increased risk of drug-drug and drug-food interactions. These interactions can affect the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of calcium channel blockers, potentially altering their efficacy and safety [1].

## Clinical Notes  
- Calcium channel blockers are commonly used for hypertension and other cardiovascular conditions in elderly patients.  
- Combination therapy, which is frequent in elderly patients, increases the risk of clinically significant drug-drug interactions involving calcium channel blockers.  
- Clinicians should carefully review all medications, supplements, and dietary habits to avoid interactions that may impact drug levels or effects.  
- Monitoring for adverse effects and therapeutic response is essential, especially when initiating or changing therapy in elderly patients.
