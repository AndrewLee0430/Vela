## Summary

Elderly patients can generally take blood pressure medications (BP meds) with calcium, but caution is required due to the potential for drug-drug and drug-food interactions, especially with calcium channel blockers. The risk of clinically significant interactions increases with combination therapy, which is common in elderly patients with cardiovascular diseases[1].
