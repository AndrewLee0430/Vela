**Interaction between Calcium and Lisinopril**

1. **Lisinopril Overview:**
   - Lisinopril is an angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor used primarily for hypertension, heart failure, and post-myocardial infarction management.
   - It works by inhibiting ACE, reducing angiotensin II levels, leading to vasodilation, decreased aldosterone secretion, and reduced blood pressure.

2. **Calcium Overview:**
   - Calcium supplements or calcium-containing antacids are commonly used for bone health or as antacids.
   - Calcium is an essential mineral involved in muscle contraction, nerve transmission, and bone metabolism.

3. **Potential Interaction:**
   - There is **no direct significant pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic interaction** between calcium supplements and lisinopril.
   - However, calcium supplements, especially calcium carbonate, can sometimes affect the absorption of certain medications if taken simultaneously.
   - Lisinopril absorption is generally not significantly affected by calcium, but it is advisable to separate the timing of calcium supplements and lisinopril by at least 2 hours to avoid any potential minor absorption interference.

4. **Clinical Considerations:**
   - Lisinopril can increase serum potassium levels (hyperkalemia risk).
   - Calcium supplements do not typically affect potassium but be cautious if the patient is also taking potassium supplements or potassium-sparing diuretics.
   - Monitor blood pressure and renal function regularly when starting or adjusting lisinopril.
   - If the patient is on calcium-containing phosphate binders (common in chronic kidney disease), monitor electrolytes closely.

5. **Summary:**
   - No major interaction between calcium and lisinopril.
   - To optimize absorption, take lisinopril and calcium supplements at different times.
   - Monitor renal function and electrolytes as per usual lisinopril therapy guidelines.
