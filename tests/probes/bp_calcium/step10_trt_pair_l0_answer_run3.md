**Interaction Between Calcium and Lisinopril**

1. **Lisinopril Overview:**
   - Lisinopril is an angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor commonly used to treat hypertension, heart failure, and to improve survival after myocardial infarction.
   - It works by inhibiting the conversion of angiotensin I to angiotensin II, leading to vasodilation and reduced blood pressure.

2. **Calcium Overview:**
   - Calcium supplements or calcium-containing antacids are often used to treat or prevent calcium deficiency or osteoporosis.
   - Calcium is also a key mineral involved in muscle contraction, nerve function, and bone health.

3. **Potential Interaction:**
   - There is no direct pharmacokinetic interaction between calcium supplements and lisinopril.
   - However, calcium supplements, especially calcium carbonate, can potentially affect blood pressure control by causing mild vasoconstriction or by affecting electrolyte balance.
   - Calcium can sometimes blunt the antihypertensive effect of ACE inhibitors like lisinopril, although this effect is generally mild and not clinically significant in most patients.
   - Patients taking lisinopril should monitor blood pressure regularly if starting or stopping calcium supplements.

4. **Clinical Considerations:**
   - Lisinopril can increase serum potassium levels (hyperkalemia risk).
   - Calcium supplements do not typically affect potassium levels but be cautious if the patient is also taking potassium supplements or potassium-sparing diuretics.
   - Monitor renal function and electrolytes periodically in patients on lisinopril, especially if they are also taking calcium supplements or other medications affecting renal function.

5. **Summary:**
   - No major interaction between calcium supplements and lisinopril.
   - Monitor blood pressure and electrolytes as per standard practice.
   - Inform patients to report symptoms such as dizziness, muscle weakness, or irregular heartbeat.
